Welcome to Ducks on Demand

Ducks on Demand is an imaginary store that celebrates the rubber duck. We made it as a small, clean test page for website analysis tools. You can look at ducks and read about them, but you cannot buy anything.

Featured ducks

    An inflatable duck as tall as a person, shown here on a park pond. A big favourite at outdoor events.

    Fifteen costume ducks on one shelf, from chefs to vampires. Great for fans who like to collect a whole set.
